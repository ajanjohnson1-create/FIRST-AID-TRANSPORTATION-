📋 Driver Requirements

All applicants must meet the following requirements to be considered for a driving position:

Background & Licensing

  • Clean background — County & Nation 7-Year Search
  • Valid California Driver's License
  • Clean Motor Vehicle Record (MVR)

Driving Record — Last 3 Years

  • No more than 1 moving violation / ticket
  • No more than 1 at-fault accident
  • No license suspensions in the last 3 years

Health & Physical

  • Clean Drug Test — 10 Panel required
  • Must be able to pass a DOT Physical examination
