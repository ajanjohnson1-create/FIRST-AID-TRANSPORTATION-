How to Get Started

Booking Requirements

  • ⏰
    48-Hour Advance Notice: We require a 48-hour notice in advance along with the patient's documentation.
  • 🚨
    Urgent Requests: We can respond quickly. Call 510-479-7328 directly.
  • 📄
    New Patient Referrals: Schedule online or call with the patient's demographic sheet and appointment details.
  • 💊
    Insurance Accepted: Medi-Cal, Contra Costa Health Plan (CCHP), HPSM, and Private Pay.
  • 🛡️
    Fully Insured: Our company carries full insurance coverage for all services.
