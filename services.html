Our Specializations

What We Transport

We specialize in the following transport modes to accommodate every patient's needs.

♿

Wheelchair Transport

Safe, reliable wheelchair transport with certified securement. Vehicles equipped with ramps and lifts for full accessibility.

🛏️

Gurney / Stretcher

Standard gurney transport for patients who are unable to sit upright or are bed-bound. Full securement and comfort measures.

⚖️

Bariatric Gurney

Specialized bariatric gurney transport for patients requiring extra accommodation. Our team is trained and equipped.

🚶

Ambulatory Transport

Comfortable rides for patients who can walk independently or with minimal assistance. Door-to-door care included.

🏠

Door-to-Door Assistance

We assist patients from their door to the vehicle and all the way to their destination — every step of the way.

🪜

Stair Assistance

Trained staff provide safe stair assistance for patients who need help navigating stairs at home or medical facilities.

💨

Oxygen Tank Vehicles

Vehicles equipped to transport patients who require oxygen tanks during transit. Safety and comfort guaranteed.
